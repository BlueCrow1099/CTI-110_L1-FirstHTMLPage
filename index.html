I've been playing a few different games recently, mostly on the PS4/5 but also on my Nintendo Switch. So here are a few of them and my current thoughts (I've finished none):

  • Persona 3: Reload (PS4): Really enjoying this game. The pacing is a bit slow, but I don't mind (even if I did, I would not be in a position to complain; Animal Crossing: New Horizons was my thing for all of seventh grade). The turn-based combat is good and, as someone who can't memorize a type system for the life of me, I appreciate the game just telling you which type moves will be effective and which won't. The soundtrack is also very good.
  • Devil May Cry 5 (PS4): In my defense of having not finished any of these games, I've almost finished this one. And in my defense of not playing the DMC games in order, I watched one too many YouTube videos about these games (some with context, some without) and pretty much know the story (but I will play the prior games anyways) (except for DMC2 and DmC, I know better than to waste time on a bad game). The hack-n-slash combat in this game is amazing, especially the air combat. The amount of different ways to increase your combos makes the game feel very dynamic, especially once you're later rewarded for said combos. And, of course, the graphics are great as well, along with the OST.
  • Batman: Arkham Knight (PS4): I have a love-hate relationship with this game. On one hand, I love the free-flow combat, the graphics in this game are amazing especially considering that it came out ~10 years ago, and the voice actors did an excellent job (RIP Kevin Conroy). It also gives my favorite DC Character, Jason Todd, some much-needed screentime, although the foreshadowing for his appearance in this series could have been better planned-out. On the other hand, however, are the tank fights, the puzzles, (haven't touched the game in months because I'm stuck on one,) and the sometimes annoying lack of guidance with side quests. (Which is probably partially my fault for picking the hardest difficulty, but what am I going to do, change it? No. No, I won't.) All of that said, I do really like the game, even when it's being really annoying.
  • Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream (Switch): I love this game. I got it for my birthday, and it is pure, unhinged chaos. The first Mii that I added was John Wick. He married Frieren. I've also added Machine Head (Ivincible), Kaneki (Tokyo Ghoul), Scarlet El Vandimion (May I Ask For One Final Thing?), Sunset Shimmer (MLP EG), Jason Todd (DC), and the Powerpuff Girls. There is no rhyme or reason to anything that happens in this game. I'm currently making a few comic pages based on a CaseOh fan meetup that some of my Miis had in the restaurant. Would recommend. I'll probably add Sasha from Attack on Titan next.
  • LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight (PS5): This game is honestly so good. Combat-wise, it plays like an Arkham game, minus the annoying tank battles. There are so many skins, each a reference to the Batman movies and comics (honestly the Btaman Beyond skins might be the closest we're going to get to a Batman Beyond game). It's also a bit like the Insomniac Spider-Man games where you can just wander around the open world and find criminals to fight. And, of course, the graphics are amazing. It also has a co-op option, so I've been playing it with my brother.

There are also a couple of games that I'm looking forward to. A lot of things were announced over the summer, so here are the ones that caught my attention the most:

  • Persona 6: I was honestly starting to doubt whether or not we'd get this. For the past 10 years Atlus and SEGA have been milking Joker and Persona 5 dry, but this summer, we got leaked concept art for persona 6 and a few days later, the teaser trailer was dropped. There wasn't too much content in the trailer, but based off of the graveyards, skulls, and green, I think that Persona 6 is going to lean more into the horror aspects of the series, which I'm very excited to see.
  • Stranger Than Heaven: A Yakuza prequel game set to come out this upcoming January, I have to admit that I've never played a Yakuza game and I don't know anything about the lore of the series. However, the combat in the trailer looked very fun, and (admittedly a tad more important to me) Ado, a J-Pop/J-Rock artist that I really like will be voice acting and singing in this game. And based off of the song in the trailer alone, (which also had Snoop Dogg, Tori Kelly, and Satoshi Fushihara,) I know that every single song in this game is going to go very hard, and it's a little too easy to convince me to get a game based on the OST alone.

Finally, some games that are out and, admittedly, been out for a while that I haven't played. But I INTEND to play them, so I'll talk about them and why I want to play them here. They're also ordered for prioritization:

  1. Resident Evil 4: I've been really wanting to get into the Resident Evil franchise, and since there are too many games and remakes of said games to play them all, let alone them all in order, I think that 4 would just be a good place to start since it has good reviews and Leon. I'm not sure if I want to play the original or remake more though, since the remake has better graphics but the original has goofier dialogue. ("No way, Leon!" "Way.")
  2. Spider-Man: Web of Shadows: I know the plot of this game. I know the story, and I've seen the combat, and my brother has it. And with all of this information, I know that I want to oplay it. It looks so fun, and I do want to experience a Spider-Man game from before the Insomniac era.
  3. DMC: I've already mentioned this before, but I do want to play through the rest of the DMC franchise. I will be skipping DMC2 though, because I've already watched reviews about it and know that the combat is lacking for a game in this franchise and due to poor writing, the plot doesn't make much sense either. I will also be skipping DmC, which is an edgy reboot of the first game, as I've watched reviews about it as well.
  4. Arkham: On a similar note, I do also want to play the rest of the Arkham games. Of course, I do also want to finish Knight before I do, so that might have to wait a while . . .
